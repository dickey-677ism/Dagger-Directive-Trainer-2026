## 🎮 About the Game

Dagger Directive is a tactical first-person shooter developed by Arcane Alpacas LLC and published by MicroProse Software. It launched on August 13, 2026, following Early Access beginning June 3, 2025. The game features single-player and online co-op missions across three campaigns set in the Bering Sea, South Pacific, and Eastern Europe.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Dagger Directive**. This trainer provides optional assistance for Dagger Directive's tactical missions, including survivability, ammunition, reloads, equipment, movement, and precision shooting. It is designed for offline single-player use and complements the game's loadouts, weapon attachments, drones, grenades, mines, stealth, and physics-based ballistics.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with loadouts, weapons, attachments, stealth routes, tactical equipment, open mission areas, and physics-based combat without the normal pressure of resource management or damage.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement options to explore large mission areas, alternate entry points, dense foliage, elevated positions, and shadowed routes. Silent Movement is useful for testing stealth approaches, while the speed multiplier can help with traversal and route planning.

### Tools & Inventory

Prepare the desired loadout before deployment, including weapons, attachments, night-vision equipment, drones, grenades, mines, and tripwires. Unlimited Equipment is intended for testing different tactical combinations without exhausting deployable tools during a mission.

### World & Environment

Missions take place in open tactical environments across three global campaigns. Time of day affects visibility and stealth, while lights, foliage, vertical positions, and multiple approaches can shape the operation. Use the trainer to inspect routes and experiment with environmental tactics.

### Combat & Weapons

Dagger Directive models projectile weight, velocity, trajectory, distance, gravity, and ricochets. Infinite Ammo and No Reload support weapon testing, while Steady Aim helps evaluate optics, attachments, long-range shots, suppressed weapons, and different engagement distances.
